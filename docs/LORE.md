# Triad: Klondike — Lore Bible (v1.0)

> Terrain: official Arma Reforger **Kolguyev** (1.6+). Ugoldar and Kamensk are the named
> settlements. Both companies are based in Ugoldar; Kamensk belongs to nobody.

## 1. The Country

**Republic of Belomorye** (Республика Беломорье) — a small state, one of the fragments
of the Soviet Union. Kolguyev is its most distant island: a few hours by sea from the
mainland, mining country since Soviet times.

- **1991** — independence with the collapse of the USSR. The republic inherits
  Kolguyev's mines, garrisons, and settlements it can barely afford to keep.
- **1990s–2000s** — the mines close one by one; the young leave for the mainland. The
  state's presence on the island shrinks to a port office, a post office, and one
  militia sergeant.
- **2014–2018** — civil war on the mainland. Four years of fighting produced no winner;
  with nothing left to fight with, the sides signed a ceasefire. The war never reached
  the island — it was simply forgotten.
- **2018–2021** — the "reconstruction": foreign loans and two defaults. By 2021 the
  treasury is empty.
- **2021–2022** — kolguyevite is found on Kolguyev. The dispute over who may dig it
  does not stay legal for long.

## 2. The Island

Kolguyev — a dormant volcano, autumn taiga, old mine workings, settlements by the water.

- **Ugoldar** — the island's biggest town. When the shooting started in the west and
  center, nearly everyone still living elsewhere moved here: it is safer, and there is
  work — the companies need housing, provisions, and hands. The town is neutral ground.
  Both companies keep their compounds here.
- **Kamensk** (north-east) — a garrison town built for a submarine base. When the Union
  broke up the boats left and the base fell into decline. For a time the Belomorye navy's
  few remaining ships were still serviced at its piers, but the unit was disbanded in the
  end: the buildings emptied, and people began to leave.

  When kolguyevite was found, a black market formed in the half-empty town of its own
  accord, and civilian ships began putting in at the piers. Granit and Meridian look the
  other way, and now and then use the locals' services themselves.
- **The volcano** (center-west) — bare slopes; on the flanks, chains of hot pools with
  mineral crusts along their rims. The crust glints blue-green.
- **The workings** (west and center) — adits, quarries, and conveyors of the closed
  mining kombinat. This is where the rock that holds kolguyevite has been laid open —
  and this is what the war is fought over.
- **The small island south of Kolguyev** — the Belomorye army garrison, the last thing
  the state has left here.

## 3. The Devil's Stone

**Kolguyevite** (колгуевит) — glassy blue-green crystals in the island's bedrock,
deposited over millennia by the volcano's geothermal brines.

- **1968** — Soviet mineralogists describe the crystal during routine surveying and
  name it after the locality. The scientists' conclusion: no industrial value.
- **1960s–1991** — to the miners it is the rock that breaks drill bits and fouls
  furnaces. When it turns up in a seam, it goes out with the waste rock. Around here
  they call it **чёртов камень** — the devil's stone.
- **2021** — physicists studying superconductivity in natural minerals eventually get
  to kolguyevite. It turns out to be a natural superconductor — a rare crystal
  structure. Materials with such properties are made in laboratories and cost more than
  gold; here the finished crystal lies in the ground.
- **2022** — the only known deposit is Kolguyev.

For half a century kolguyevite was treated as waste rock. Today a gram of clean crystal
is worth more than a miner here earned in a year.

## 4. The Concessions

The digging rights went first to **Концерн «Гранит» / Granit Concern** — the way such
questions had always been settled in Belomorye: in offices.

Half a year later the government fell. Street protests, snap elections — the opposition
openly backed by the West. The new cabinet announced a review of the old licenses and
granted extraction rights to **Meridian Group**. Granit's license was never actually
revoked: the process sank in the courts.

So by law both companies hold the right to mine — and neither intends to yield.

- **Granit Concern** — an industrial conglomerate grown out of the Soviet mining
  industry; its capital comes from across the former Union. It brought old military
  hardware to the island, and men accustomed to undeclared wars.
- **Meridian Group** — a Western mining corporation: press releases about "responsible
  extraction", lawyers, contractors. On the island its interests are defended by hired
  guns.

The states behind the companies cannot afford to fight each other — so the fighting is
left to hired men. Both corporations keep private military contractors recruited to the
same pattern: ex-soldiers, easy-money chasers, criminals — people with nowhere to go
back to. Officially they guard the mining sites. In practice they fight a war that never
makes the news.

## 5. The Locals

Nobody asked the islanders. A few thousand people: fishermen, former miners, and their
families.

When the shooting started in the island's west and center, people moved to Ugoldar —
it is safer there, and the companies pay well.

But neutrality is not obedience. The island is their home, and the foreign war is a
living. Some work as guides, some trade, and some go out to the workings at night —
they cut at another man's face with another man's tools and are gone before the trucks
come, or they simply take what a company man has already cut and cannot carry away.
The black market takes everything — stolen kolguyevite, weapons, gear stripped from the
dead. In Ugoldar it is bought quietly; in Kamensk it is sold back, most often to the
same companies.

The companies do the same to each other and call it competition.

*(Gameplay faction: **Locals** / «Местные» — raiders and poachers, holding no mines.)*

## 6. The Garrison

The Belomorye army holds the small island south of Kolguyev. It cannot expel the
companies: the licenses are genuine, and the republic has nothing to fight two private
armies with.

But the presence turned out to be profitable. Both companies pay the state for
extraction — and pay the army command separately: for clearances, and for patrols that
happen to be elsewhere at the right time. For the army command this conflict is the
best thing to happen in all their years of service.

Nobody touches the Garrison: everyone needs it exactly as it is.

*(Gameplay: whitelisted faction — admins, moderators, media/streamers.)*

## 7. The Island Keeps It

Grey dawns, wet concrete, rusted cranes against a white sky. There are no heroes here:
everyone came for the money, and everyone suspects the money will stay with the island.

Work on Kolguyev runs in shifts — the season here is simply called the **Vakhta**
(вахта). You fly in, you work your shift, you fly out. The island stays — and waits
for the next shift.

> The old men spent their lives throwing this stone away.
> Now people kill for it.
> The old men say: so we were right to throw it.
